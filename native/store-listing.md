## App Review notes (iOS)
Sign-in is optional; the full game is playable without an account. Sign-in opens our website in Safari (Google or e-mail code). Chat is limited to friends added by friend code, with block and report in every conversation. Coins are a consumable in-app purchase (em_coins_500). Ads: AdMob app-open, interstitial (every 5 levels) and optional rewarded ads.

## Data safety / App privacy
- Collected only with an account: user ID (random), display name, game progress, friend list, chat messages (90 days), e-mail address hashed (never stored in plain text).
- Device / advertising data: collected by Google AdMob for ads and measurement; consent via Google UMP, iOS ATT prompt.
- Purchases: handled by Google Play / Apple; we do not receive payment details.
- Data is encrypted in transit; users can delete their account in the app or at /hesap-silme.
